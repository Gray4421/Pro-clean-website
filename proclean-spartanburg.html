SERVICES

What gets cleaned

If it sits outside and collects mildew, pollen, or years of dirt, chances are Pro-Clean has already washed one just like it.

House Washing

Low-pressure soft wash for siding, brick, and trim — lifts mildew and algae without damaging paint or surfaces.

Driveways & Concrete

Strips ground-in dirt, oil stains, and black mildew streaks from driveways, walkways, and patios.

Roof Cleaning

Soft-wash roof treatments that remove black streaking without the high-pressure damage a garden-variety wash can cause.

Decks & Fencing

Restores wood and composite decking, rails, and fence lines ahead of staining or just to bring the color back.

RVs & Campers

Exterior wash for campers and travel trailers — road grime and mildew off in one visit.

Commercial & Sand Blasting

Larger-scale cleaning for businesses, churches, and institutions, plus sand blasting for tougher surfaces.
